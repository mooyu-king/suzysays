01 / LISTEN CLOSELY

A simple rule. A very different game.

The most important Suzy Says habit is to listen to the beginning of the sentence. A color, movement, or object can sound familiar while the instruction is deliberately missing its required prefix. Pause long enough to understand the whole line before deciding whether to act.

Suzy Says also distinguishes between actions. Looking at a picture is not necessarily the same as touching it. Community guides recommend paying attention to the mat and returning as directed. If an action fails, check the wording and your position before assuming the game needs restarting.

02 / BEHIND THE SMILE

Inside the Suzy Says house.

Suzy Says turns a familiar listening game into an uneasy evening at home. An interactive DVD introduces a friendly television host, but her instructions gradually reach beyond the screen. The contrast between playful colors and an increasingly uncomfortable house gives the experience its distinctive mood.

This Suzy Says guide starts with the information you need before a first attempt. Keep the full walkthrough closed if you want to discover the story yourself. When you need help, choose your current stage and reveal one hint at a time instead of reading the entire ending.

05 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Suzy Says questions, answered.

What is Suzy Says?+

Suzy Says is a first-person horror game built around an interactive DVD and a colorful floor mat. A cheerful host gives instructions while an ordinary home becomes increasingly unsettling. Listening carefully matters more than reacting to the last word you hear.

Can I play Suzy Says on this page?+

Press Play Suzy Says in the game window to load the embedded build. Click inside the player before using your keyboard. Loading depends on your connection and browser. Keep the controls, stage hints, videos, and FAQ nearby; reloading the game may lose progress.

What are the Suzy Says controls?+

The published desktop controls are WASD to move, Space to jump, and C to crouch. Look toward objects with the game camera. Follow the interaction prompts in your version rather than assuming that every object uses the same key.

Why does Suzy Says punish an action that looks correct?+

Check the prefix, the requested action, and your position first. Looking at an object is different from touching it. Community reports also describe inputs that do not register, so not every failed action is necessarily a misunderstanding of the rules.

Does Suzy Says work on a phone?+

Touch controls for the embedded player on this site have not been verified. These guide pages work on small screens, but that does not prove the game itself supports touch. Check the player support note before planning a mobile session.

How many endings does Suzy Says have?+

Community guides group the outcomes differently. A ritual outcome and a secret route are discussed, while being caught and destroying the disc are classified inconsistently. Our endings guide describes actions and version limits instead of presenting disputed names as an official count.

What is the Suzy Says secret code?+

This answer is a spoiler. One detailed community guide reports red, yellow, red, green, blue, yellow, learned through the living-room radio. The secret route belongs to the Full download version. Its availability in the embedded browser player remains unverified.

Where are all four candles in Suzy Says?+

A complete set of verified locations is not available in our research. One community guide points to the bathroom near the toilet, but does not confirm the other three. We will not label an invented room map as a complete solution.

How long does Suzy Says take?+

Public playthroughs commonly run around seventeen to twenty minutes. Exploration, retries, and optional routes can change that. Treat the estimate as a planning aid, not a timer or a promise that every player will finish at the same speed.

Does this site save my game?+

The guide can remember which walkthrough sections you marked as read on this device. That is reading progress, not a game save. Clearing the guide checklist does not clear game data. Browser-player saving will need a separate compatibility check.

Suzy Says includes flashing imagery, sudden scares, and unsettling themes. Read at your own pace, keep your volume comfortable, and take a break when needed. This independent guide is based on published information and community walkthroughs; it does not claim that every route has been tested in the upcoming player.

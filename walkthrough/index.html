01 · SETUP

Coming home

GENTLE HINT

Take care of the ordinary things first.

A more specific hint

Put your school bag away, then look for the kitchen note and the game box.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

Community walkthroughs describe bringing the DVD and floor mat back to the bedroom. Follow the interaction prompts to set up the television. Check the room before you begin: knowing the way out will help later.

02 · PRACTICE

Learn the rule

GENTLE HINT

Listen for her name before you move.

A more specific hint

The reported practice includes red, green, an unprefixed blue instruction, and yellow. Ignore the unprefixed instruction.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

Treat the practice as a listening lesson, not a sequence to memorize for the whole game. Later instructions can vary. Wait for the complete line, check its prefix, and then perform the requested action.

03 · ERRANDS

Explore the rooms

GENTLE HINT

A familiar object is your next landmark.

A more specific hint

The sunflower painting is in the living room, the rubber duck is in the bathroom, and the drawing is on the kitchen fridge.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

Read whether the instruction asks you to look at an object or touch it. Follow the current return instruction and be ready for the next line. Avoid treating every mention of a familiar object as permission to leave the mat.

04 · INTERRUPTION

The visitor

GENTLE HINT

A distraction does not replace the rules.

A more specific hint

Community accounts describe an open front door, a phone call, and changes to the lights.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

Follow the current objective rather than reacting to every sound. The sequence can be unsettling, but a sudden movement on screen is not itself a command. Exact intermediate instructions should be checked in your version.

05 · STAY OUT OF SIGHT

Hide and seek

GENTLE HINT

Break the line of sight.

A more specific hint

Community guides suggest leaving exposed areas, using furniture for cover, and crouching.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

Wait for the hiding phase to finish before returning. No particular hiding spot or duration is guaranteed here. Reports disagree on whether being caught is a separate ending or a retry state, so treat it as a failed attempt rather than a confirmed ending route.

06 · FINAL STRETCH

Candles and choices

GENTLE HINT

Decide whether you want to follow the ritual.

A more specific hint

The main route asks for four candles. Secret-route guides suggest checking the radio before completing this objective.

Show detailed steps · spoilers

For the ritual route, community guides describe placing the candles at the mat and following the symbols instruction. We do not have a verified map of all four candle locations. A bathroom location near the toilet is a community lead, not a complete location guide.

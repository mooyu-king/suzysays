Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Site analytics

The site includes a Plausible analytics integration served from data.1back.link, with events sent to data.1back.link/api/send. It is enabled when a production domain is configured and matches the current host. It helps us understand page visits and use of features such as guide help and installation. Requests may contain ordinary browser, page, and network information. We do not send your checklist contents, search text, game saves, or personal files as event properties.

Preferences on your device

Walkthrough reading marks are stored in your browser's local storage. The checklist reset clears only this guide's reading marks. It does not delete game saves or unrelated site data. Storage may be unavailable in some private browsing modes; the checklist still works for the current page.

Videos and external services

YouTube players are loaded only when you choose to watch a video. The embed uses youtube-nocookie.com, but interacting with it still connects your browser to a third-party service that may process device and network data. Direct video links open YouTube. Pressing Play connects to s.anomalouscoffeemachine3.com, which hosts the game and may receive network and browser information or store game data. Game storage is separate from this guide checklist.

Installed app and offline content

A service worker caches this site's guide pages and assets to help with repeat visits. Third-party games, YouTube videos, and analytics are not added to the offline cache. Installation does not make an external game available offline. You can remove stored site content through browser settings.

Contact and choices

Contacting us by email shares the information you choose to send and the ordinary information associated with email delivery. Please do not include passwords, sensitive personal information, or private game data. Privacy questions can be sent to mooyuking@gmail.com.

Endings, without the guesswork.

Outcome names differ between community guides. Compare the actions behind each route, and open the spoilers deliberately.

The published Full download includes a secret ending and Endless Mode. The embedded player on this website has not been checked for those features.

01

The ritual route

Often called True, Normal, or Good in community guides. Those names do not mean the outcome is happy.

Reveal the reported outcome

Reports describe gathering four candles, placing them at the mat, and following the symbols instruction. The sequence leads to the television taking the player. Exact placement and version details should be checked in your game.

02

The disc-related route

Some accounts separate disc destruction from the secret ending; others describe them together.

Reveal the reported action

Removing and breaking the DVD appears in community descriptions. Whether it can be done directly, requires an earlier completion, or depends on radio events has not been consistently established. Do not assume that breaking the disc alone guarantees the secret outcome.

03

The secret route

A reported sequence connects the living-room radio, the mat, and the DVD. Full-version feature.

Reveal the community sequence

One detailed guide reports listening during the introduction, after closing the front door, after the phone call, after hiding, and after the candle request. The final message supplies a color sequence.

  1. Red
  2. Yellow
  3. Red
  4. Green
  5. Blue
  6. Yellow

That account describes jumping on those colors, then removing and breaking the DVD after an error appears. Whether every broadcast is mandatory or the sequence works without hearing it is unverified.

Does getting caught count as an ending?

Sources disagree. Some label it a bad ending, while another describes a failed attempt followed by a retry. We treat it as a failure outcome without adding it to a supposedly official ending count.

What is confirmed, and what is still a report?

The secret ending and Endless Mode are published Full-version features. The six-color sequence and radio timing come from a detailed community guide. This site has not independently completed that sequence in the embedded browser player.

Accounts also speculate about the radio speaker. The character's identity should not be presented as confirmed story information. A theory can be interesting without becoming a fact.

The prefix comes first

Listen for “Suzy says” before acting. If it is absent, leave the instruction alone. Looking and touching are not interchangeable, and a house errand can be a trap just like a color command. Follow the current interaction prompts; a universal interaction key has not been confirmed here.

Before you restart

Check the sentence, your position, and the action. Some community reports link failed inputs to standing away from the mat. Other reports describe apparent input bugs, so a failed action is not automatically your fault.

The launch patch documented a subtitle correction, not a general input fix. Reports of a fourth candle not placing do not come with a verified solution. Do not repeatedly restart expecting an undocumented fix to work.

If a browser player has no sound or input, check its own settings and focus first. Restarting may lose progress. The embedded player uses a separate service. Click inside its window to focus input; touch support remains unverified.

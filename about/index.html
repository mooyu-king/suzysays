About this guide

A companion for curious players

This is an independent guide to Suzy Says. It brings controls, optional hints, selected videos, and frequently asked questions together in one place. It is not presented as the developer's official website.

How we handle uncertain information

Published platform and control information is separated from community walkthrough advice. Where sources disagree, we describe the disagreement instead of inventing a precise answer. A route described in a desktop guide may not match a browser build.

Images and the player

The screenshots show the game and remain the property of their respective rights holders. The television-character website badge is a newly designed illustration, not the official game logo. The game area loads an externally hosted player when you press Play.

Corrections

If a command, route, or version note needs correcting, please contact us with the relevant page and enough detail to locate the issue. We do not claim that every branch has been independently played.
